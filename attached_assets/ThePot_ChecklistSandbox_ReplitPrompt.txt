## ✅ CORE UPDATE: ADD A CHECKLIST WALKTHROUGH

### 1. Checklist Interface
Create a UI component called `FeatureChecklist.jsx` that appears when users launch the Sandbox MVP.

Checklist Items:
- [ ] Create a Pot and Invite Members
- [ ] Deposit into an Active Pot
- [ ] Request a Withdrawal (if conditions met)
- [ ] Request a Goal Change in an Active Pot
- [ ] Cast Your Vote on a Withdrawal Request

Each item is:
- Initially unchecked
- Becomes checked when the action is completed
- Clickable to highlight where in the app to go or to open the related scenario modal

### 2. Scenario-Based Pot Creation Flow
When users click “Create a Pot,” prompt them to choose from one of these preset use cases:

**Use Cases + Example Scenarios:**
- **Bills / Rent** → “You and 4 students share a townhouse, splitting Netflix & Prime bills ($30 total split 5 ways).”
- **Trip Planning** → “You and 3 friends are booking an Airbnb and decorations for a celebration ($1,200 split 4 ways).”
- **Gift Collaboration** → “You and 2 coworkers want to buy a $150 gift basket for your boss.”
- **Team Fundraiser** → “A basketball team needs $500 for jerseys. Everyone contributes.”
- **Club Dues / Deposit** → “Your university club collects $25/month from 10 members.”

Offer the user:
- “Start Pot with This Scenario” (pre-fills Pot form and use case)
- OR “Create Your Own Custom Pot for This Category”
